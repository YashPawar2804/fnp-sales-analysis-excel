# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data for FNP (Ferns N Petals) using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to track sales performance, revenue distribution, customer spending, product performance, and order trends.

The dashboard presents key business metrics and visualizations to help understand sales patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Businesses need to understand which products and occasions generate the most revenue, when customers place orders, and which cities contribute significantly to sales.

This project analyzes sales data to identify revenue trends, top-performing product categories, customer ordering patterns, and sales distribution across different occasions and locations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and order volume.
- Evaluate revenue generated across different occasions.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Analyze order distribution across cities.
- Understand monthly revenue trends and seasonal patterns.
- Identify the top 5 products by revenue.
- Analyze revenue by order hour.
- Track average customer spending and order-to-delivery time.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Revenue by Occasion
Analyzes revenue generated from different occasions, including Anniversary, Birthday, Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, Valentine's Day, and All Occasions.

### 2. Revenue by Category
Compares revenue across product categories such as Colors, Mugs, Plants, Cakes, Raksha Bandhan, Soft Toys, and Sweets.

### 3. Top 10 Cities by Orders
Highlights cities with the highest order volumes to understand geographical sales distribution.

### 4. Revenue by Month
Displays monthly revenue trends to identify high-performing months and seasonal fluctuations.

### 5. Top 5 Products by Revenue
Identifies the products generating the highest revenue.

### 6. Revenue by Hour
Analyzes revenue based on order time to understand customer purchasing patterns throughout the day.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- **Occasion-based revenue:** Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan are among the highest revenue-generating occasions in the dashboard.
- **Category performance:** Colors is the leading revenue category, followed by Soft Toys and Sweets.
- **Monthly trends:** February and August show notable revenue peaks, suggesting strong seasonal or occasion-driven sales.
- **City performance:** Dhanbad, Imphal, and Kavali show comparatively high order volumes among the cities displayed.
- **Product performance:** The dashboard highlights the top 5 revenue-generating products, helping identify products contributing significantly to sales.
- **Order-time trends:** Revenue fluctuates throughout the day, with noticeable peaks during evening hours.

## 💼 Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-revenue occasions when planning promotional campaigns and seasonal marketing.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for top-performing product categories.
- Use monthly sales trends to support demand forecasting and inventory planning.
- Explore city-wise order patterns to identify opportunities for targeted marketing.
- Promote high-revenue products while monitoring the performance of other product categories.
- Use order-time trends to optimize marketing campaigns and customer engagement timing.
